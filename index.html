Publication & Presentation

Peer-Reviewed Research

Harnessing Wearable Technology for Predictive Health Scoring: A Comparative Study of ML Models

  • JournalInternational Journal of Advance and Applied Research (IJAAR) · ISSN 2347-7075 · Double-Blind Peer-Reviewed
  • AuthorsSupriya Sapa, Prof. Dr. Sharvari C. Tamane, Vibhuti Nair
  • Research TimelineMay 2025 – June 2025
  • ConferenceNational Conference on Recent Trends in AI and ML (NCERT AIML 2026) · Mar 13–14, 2026
  • PublishedApril 2026

Co-authored and published in the peer-reviewed IJAAR, this research transforms raw biometric telemetry into actionable predictive health metrics. I built an end-to-end analytical pipeline to quantitatively compare multiple ML architectures — Support Vector Regression (SVR), XGBoost, and Gradient Boosting — with my primary contribution in feature engineering workflows and rigorous model evaluation protocols benchmarking predictive accuracy. Systematically isolating signal from noise in high-frequency sensor data, the work demonstrates how ensemble learning can deliver precise, quantitative health scoring for non-invasive remote patient monitoring.
